- DXF underlay import now expands `BLOCK`/`INSERT` geometry, including insertion base points, rotation, nonuniform scale, nested inserts and rectangular insert arrays, alongside model-space `LINE`, `LWPOLYLINE`, polyline-boundary `HATCH` (including bulge arcs), `CIRCLE` and `ARC`. DWG and external references remain unsupported; DXF geometry itself does not infer survey provenance.
